Privacy Policy

Personal Gmail Drafts. Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Who uses it

This is a private tool used only by its developer, James Carr, for his own Gmail account. No one else is permitted to sign in.

What it accesses

With James's permission it reads Gmail messages and creates or updates drafts. It cannot send email, delete messages, or change labels.

What is stored

Email content is not stored. The only data kept is an encrypted sign-in token that lets the tool keep working without asking James to sign in again.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising.

Limited Use

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs by Personal Gmail Drafts will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Open your Google Account, then Security, then Third-party access, select Personal Gmail Drafts and choose Remove access. You can also email james.carr001@gmail.com.
