Personal Gmail Drafts

Personal Gmail Drafts is a private tool used only by its developer, James Carr, with his own Gmail account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

The tool lets James's AI assistant read messages in his Gmail account and create or update email drafts there. It reads and drafts only. It cannot send email, delete anything, or change labels.

Your data

The tool does not store email content. The only thing kept is an encrypted sign-in token. No data is shared or sold.
